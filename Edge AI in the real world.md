###  Notes
#### <b><u>Greenfield Projects</u></b>
- project conditions are new or untouched and unexplored
- risky and costly
#### <b><u>Brownfield Projects</u></b>
- you have an existing framework
#### <b><u>Real-world Products</u></b>
- Power line monitoring
	- monitor sensor to measure cable length to prevent it from drooping too low
- Wearable
	- golf watch that detects grip pressure, backswing, and impact point
		- gives feedback to the player on their swing
#### <b><u>Responsible Design and AI ethics</u></b>
- Bias
- Discrimination
- Fairness
	- hard to define due to individual cultures and backgrounds
#### <b><u>Closing Thoughts</u></b>
- Try not to learn everything or you'll spread yourself too thin
- "silo project" where you've built something from top to bottom
