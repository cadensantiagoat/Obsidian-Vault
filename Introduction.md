###  Notes
#### <b><u>What is Edge AI</u></b>
##### Key Terms
- Embedded systems (microcontrollers/microprocessors)
	- microcontrollers/microprocessors is what it was called in the past
	- next it was called Internet of Things
- Edge vs. Cloud
	- Cloud computing has the computations done on massive servers
		- reliability isn't guaranteed 
	- Edge computing is where the computations are done on the device itself
		- smaller, cheaper devices that have pretty good computation capability
- AI (Artificial Intelligence/Neural Networks)
	- Field has existed for more than 50 years
	- this class focuses on the <u>neural networks</u>
		- mimics the function of the human brain
